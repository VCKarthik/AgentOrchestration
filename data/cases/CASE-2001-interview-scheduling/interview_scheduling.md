# Interview Scheduling & Approval Policy

## Purpose
Govern the scheduling of candidate interviews and the approvals required before
extending a hire request, ensuring panel composition, compliance, and budget
alignment across the organisation.

## Approval Thresholds (by annual total compensation)

| Annual Total Compensation | Required Approver |
|---|---|
| < $100,000 | Recruiting Manager |
| $100,000 – $150,000 | Recruiting Director |
| $150,000 – $200,000 | CHRO |
| > $200,000 | CHRO + CFO |
| > $350,000 (executive hire) | CEO sign-off required |

## Required Panel Composition
All interview cases must include:
- Hiring manager from the requesting department
- One peer-level interviewer from the same team
- One cross-functional interviewer (skip-level or partner team)
- For senior roles (Director+): one executive panel member
- For external candidates at any level: at least one HR-led behavioural round

## Risk Triggers — Mandatory Escalation
- Candidate has prior employment dispute with the company
- Internal candidate transferring across business units without manager approval
- Compensation package exceeds the role's salary band by more than 15%
- Background check missing or incomplete prior to final round
- Role classified as security-sensitive (PII, financial data, system admin)
- Candidate referred by a senior executive (VP or above)

## SLA
- Standard interview scheduling: 5 business days from request to first round
- Expedited scheduling: 2 business days (Recruiting Director approval required)
- Background check completion: required before final round
- Offer extended: within 3 business days of final round

## Compliance
All interview decisions must comply with EEOC equal opportunity guidelines, the
company's structured interview rubric, and internal Compensation Banding Policy
v2.1. No offer may be extended without documented panel scorecards and a
calibration review for senior hires.
