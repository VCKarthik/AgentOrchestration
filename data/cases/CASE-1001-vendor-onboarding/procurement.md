# Procurement & Vendor Onboarding Policy

## Purpose
Govern the onboarding of new vendors and procurement decisions across the organization.

## Approval Thresholds

| Contract Value | Required Approver |
|---|---|
| < $10,000 | Department Manager |
| $10,000 – $100,000 | Procurement Director |
| > $100,000 | CFO + Procurement Director |
| > $500,000 | Board approval required |

## Required Documentation
All vendor onboarding cases must include:
- Signed vendor agreement
- Certificate of insurance (minimum $1M liability)
- W-9 or equivalent tax form
- Reference check (minimum 2 references for contracts > $50k)
- Security questionnaire (for vendors with system access)

## Risk Triggers — Mandatory Escalation
- Vendor has active regulatory investigation
- Vendor country on OFAC sanctions list
- Missing insurance certificate at time of contract execution
- Contract value exceeds approved budget line
- Vendor provides access to PII or financial data without signed DPA

## SLA
- Standard onboarding: 10 business days
- Expedited onboarding: 3 business days (Director approval required)
- Missing documents must be flagged within 24 hours of case creation

## Compliance
All procurement decisions must comply with SOX Section 404, GDPR Article 28 (data processing agreements), and internal Vendor Risk Management Policy v3.2.
