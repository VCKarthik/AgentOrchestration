# Claims Review Policy

## Purpose
Define the criteria and process for reviewing, approving, and escalating insurance and reimbursement claims.

## Claim Categories

| Type | Max Auto-Approval | Review Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Medical reimbursement | $500 | > $500 requires manager sign-off |
| Equipment claim | $2,000 | > $2,000 requires department head |
| Liability claim | $0 (all require review) | Legal must review all |
| Property damage | $5,000 | > $5,000 requires Risk committee |

## Required Documentation
- Claim form (AMP-CLM-01) completed and signed
- Proof of incident (photos, police report, medical records as applicable)
- Original receipts or invoices
- Third-party statements where applicable (incidents involving external parties)

## Risk Triggers — Mandatory Escalation
- Claimant has filed more than 3 claims in the past 12 months
- Claim submitted more than 30 days after incident date
- Discrepancy between claim amount and supporting documentation > 15%
- Claim involves potential fraud indicators (inconsistent dates, altered documents)
- Claim amount exceeds $25,000
- Claimant is a related party or has a conflict of interest

## SLA
- Acknowledgement: within 2 business days of submission
- Initial review: within 5 business days
- Final decision: within 15 business days (standard) / 5 business days (expedited)
- Disputed decisions: must be appealed within 30 days

## Compliance
Claims processing must comply with state insurance regulations, internal Fraud Prevention Policy FP-2024, and applicable policy terms. All decisions must be documented in the AMP system with a rationale.
