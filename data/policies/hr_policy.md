# HR Escalation Policy

## Purpose
Define the protocols for handling sensitive HR matters including whistleblower reports, misconduct allegations, grievances, and workplace investigations.

## Case Classification
| Case Type | Description | Response Time | Escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whistleblower Report | Alleged policy or legal violation reported confidentially | 24 hours | HR Director + Legal Counsel |
| Misconduct Allegation | Alleged breach of code of conduct by an employee | 48 hours | HR Director |
| Grievance | Formal complaint by an employee against another party | 5 business days | HR Manager |
| Investigation | Formal inquiry into alleged misconduct or policy breach | Immediate | HR Director + Legal Counsel |

## Mandatory Escalation — All Sensitive HR Cases
All cases of type hr_escalation require mandatory review by the HR Director and Legal Counsel before any action is taken. No HR escalation case may be resolved by automation or delegation alone.

## Required Actions Upon Receipt
1. Acknowledge receipt to the reporter within 24 hours (maintaining confidentiality)
2. Place a legal hold on all records related to the subject party
3. Assign a case investigator with no prior relationship to the subject
4. Notify HR Director and Legal Counsel within 1 business hour
5. Document all actions in the case audit trail — no verbal-only communications

## Confidentiality Requirements
- Case details must not be disclosed to the subject party until Legal Counsel advises
- All case files are privileged — restricted to the investigation team
- External legal or forensic support may be engaged at HR Director discretion
- Whistleblower identity must be protected; retaliation is a terminable offence

## Risk Triggers
- Allegation involves a senior manager (VP level or above) — notify General Counsel
- Allegation involves a member of the HR or Legal teams — notify external counsel
- Allegation may constitute a criminal offence — notify General Counsel immediately
- Cross-jurisdictional matter — obtain local legal advice before proceeding

## SLA
- Initial acknowledgement: within 24 hours
- Preliminary assessment: within 3 business days
- Investigation completion: within 30 business days (standard) / 10 days (expedited with legal direction)
- Outcome communication to reporter: within 5 business days of case closure

## Compliance
All HR escalation cases must comply with applicable employment law, whistleblower protection statutes, GDPR (for personal data processed during investigation), and the organization's Code of Conduct v4.0.
