# Financial Approval Policy

## Purpose
Define the authorization framework for budget reallocations, expenditure approvals, and financial commitments across all business units.

## Approval Thresholds
| Transaction Value | Required Approver |
|---|---|
| < $10,000 | Department Manager |
| $10,000 – $50,000 | Finance Manager |
| $50,000 – $100,000 | Finance Director |
| > $100,000 | CFO |
| > $500,000 | CFO + Board Finance Committee |

## Covered Transaction Types
- Budget reallocations between cost centres
- Unplanned expenditure requests
- Capital expenditure approvals
- Vendor payment authorizations outside PO
- Emergency spend requests

## Required Documentation
- Budget reallocation form (AMP-FIN-01) signed by requestor
- Business justification memo
- Finance Manager pre-approval (where applicable)
- Supporting invoices or quotes
- Budget variance analysis (for reallocations > $25,000)

## Risk Triggers — Mandatory Escalation
- Transaction creates a budget overrun in the originating cost centre
- Funds originate from a restricted or ring-fenced budget line
- Payment is to a new or unvetted beneficiary
- Transaction value is within 10% of an approval tier boundary (structuring check)
- Requestor has had a prior financial irregularity flag in the past 24 months

## SLA
- Acknowledgement: within 4 business hours
- Standard approval: within 2 business days
- Expedited approval: within 4 business hours (Director pre-authorization required)
- Rejected requests must be communicated with written rationale within 1 business day

## Compliance
All financial approvals must comply with SOX Section 302/404, internal Financial Controls Policy FC-2024, and applicable budget governance frameworks.
