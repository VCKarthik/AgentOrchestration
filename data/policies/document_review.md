# Document Review Policy

## Purpose
Establish review standards for document_review cases, including contract renewals,
policy conflict analyses, and supplier agreement reviews.

## Scope
Applies to all document_review case types submitted via AMP workflow.

## Approval Thresholds

| Review Scope Value | Action | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| > $5,000 | ESCALATE | legal_counsel |
| > $20,000 | ESCALATE | general_counsel |

## Review Requirements
All document review cases must:
- Be assigned to a qualified legal or compliance reviewer
- Produce a written conflict summary when policy conflicts are found
- Be completed within the SLA defined by urgency level

## Processing Steps
1. CSA retrieves all relevant documents and prior review history
2. RRA assesses risk of identified conflicts or ambiguous clauses
3. GPA evaluates scope value against this policy
4. IAA recommends next action (approve_documents or flag_for_legal_review)
5. DA assigns to appropriate reviewer based on scope and risk level
