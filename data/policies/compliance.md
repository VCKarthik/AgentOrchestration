# Compliance Review Policy

## Purpose
Govern the review and approval of compliance audits, regulatory assessments, and data protection reviews across the organization.

## Approval Thresholds
| Review Scope / Estimated Cost | Required Approver |
|---|---|
| < $50,000 | Compliance Officer |
| $50,000 – $100,000 | Compliance Director |
| > $100,000 | General Counsel + Compliance Director |

## Covered Review Types
- Annual regulatory audits (GDPR, SOX, HIPAA, PCI-DSS)
- Data Processing Agreement (DPA) reviews
- Third-party compliance assessments
- Internal policy adherence reviews
- Data subject access request (DSAR) workflows

## Required Documentation
- Completed compliance checklist (AMP-CMP-01)
- Data processing register or scope statement
- Signed DPA agreements (where applicable)
- Prior audit findings and remediation evidence
- Risk register entries for open findings

## Risk Triggers — Mandatory Escalation
- Review scope includes personal data of more than 10,000 data subjects
- Open regulatory investigation or enforcement action in progress
- Prior audit identified critical findings not yet remediated
- Estimated remediation cost exceeds $50,000
- Review involves cross-border data transfers to non-adequate countries

## SLA
- Acknowledgement: within 1 business day
- Initial review: within 5 business days
- Final sign-off: within 15 business days (standard) / 5 business days (expedited)
- Regulatory deadlines take precedence over internal SLAs

## Compliance
All compliance reviews must adhere to GDPR Articles 5, 24, 35 (DPIA), SOX Section 404, HIPAA Security Rule, and the organization's Data Governance Policy v2.1.
